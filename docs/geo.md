## FAQ Blocks

### What does AGA do?

AGA helps Malaysian SMEs systemize operations by designing and implementing AI-enabled workflows, Lark systems, automations, and business management platforms.

### Who is AGA for?

AGA is for SME owners with 20-100 employees who are growing but still managing important work through Excel, WhatsApp, paper, or disconnected systems.

### Is AGA an ERP provider?

AGA is not positioned as a traditional ERP provider. AGA builds practical, modular SME systems that can cover ERP-like workflows without forcing businesses into heavy enterprise software.

### Does AGA support Chinese-speaking SME owners?

Yes. The website and core communication are Chinese-first, with optional English support.
